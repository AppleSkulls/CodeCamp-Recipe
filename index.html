The perfect Recipe for getting into content creation

It's 2026 and the top job everyone in the world wants is to become a creator. Weather it is for TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube there has never been a better, and eaiser time to gear up and become start creating content. While getting into this space is something that can be very caustly You have more than enough things to get started now!

Ingredients

  • A camera
  • a place to record
  • Something to prop your phone up
  • Light! Lots and lots of light
  • Editing Softwhere
  • You!

Instructions

  1. Find your camera, This can be an older DSLR, even your phone! Make sure it can record video. As long as it can, you are good to go!
  2. Find where you want to record your video. This can be litterly anywhere from a public place like a park, in your car, renting out a studio for the day, to your room. Just be sure you are comfertible in there.
  3. Time to set up your camera. Wether your recording in 16x9 (horizontal) or 9x16 (virtical) you will need a way to prop up your camera. Normally this is where you would use a tripod, but if you dont have one stacking up some books on a desk or table is a great way to prop up your camera, without needing to shell out some of that doe. There is also the route of going hand held as well!
  4. Once the camera is set up this is where you want to check to see how it looks. If the place you are recording in is too dark your video might not look the best. You can always add more light by turning on some lamps, opening a window. If your unable to really add light now don't worry about it too much and just record! in time you can upgrade but its best to before you have everything you need!
  5. This is where the most important part comes in and its... You! Without you, there would be no video. So get in front of that camera and talk about that book you just read. Or get your hands dirty in the kitchen and make that cake. Talk about that recent announcement that (insert company here) made and why you LOVE/HATE it.
  6. Finally the editing softwhere. The ting that is kindof a the scariest sounding thing out there. The good news is there are plenty of free options! Weather you are on your phone, Capcut is a great option to get started, and is really easy to use. Or on your desktop then hands down Davinchi Resolve is truely the only choice. There are plenty of videos out there on YouTube to learn how to use eaither of these products. But the pest part is they are both free!
  7. Lastly and most importantly. Have fun
